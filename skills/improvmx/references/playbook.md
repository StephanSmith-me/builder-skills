# ImprovMX playbook

Playbook: https://stephansmith.me/tools-i-use/entry/improvmx/
Vendor: https://improvmx.com

The decision is in `SKILL.md`. This file is the detail for the free alias plan and the paid plan. Do not add a third plan. Do not invent DNS records.

Use only these sections of the public page: When to reach for it, Watchouts.

## Free aliases

Use this when one off-brand domain needs addresses such as `hello@thatdomain` in the Gmail they already use.

- The domain gets MX records so ImprovMX can accept mail for it.
- Each alias forwards into that Gmail account. Gmail stays the inbox.
- Free covers aliases. Do not recommend a paid Google Workspace, Outlook, or extra Gmail account for the domain.

## Paid plan

Use this when they have a portfolio of domains, about 30 or more, or they need redirect as well as aliases.

- One paid ImprovMX account holds the portfolio. Do not buy a mailbox seat per domain.
- The public page describes the upgrade as the point where those domains can send as well as receive, about $8 a month. Treat "redirect" as that paid step. Do not invent a different price.
- DNS is once per domain. After the first domain, the rest of the portfolio is the same move.

## Also notice

- Some ImprovMX screens are not obvious on day one. Say that, then still recommend the alias. Do not switch them to Workspace because a screen is unfamiliar.
- Do not change DNS, and do not sign them up, until they say to.
- Counting whether application code sends mail is a different question. Do not open the repo for it.
